( 04 — Questions )

FAQ.

Which Shopify plans do you work with?

All of them. Store builds, theme work, custom apps and integrations work on any Shopify plan. Some checkout customisations, such as extensions on the information, shipping and payment steps, are only available on Shopify Plus, so we confirm what your plan supports before we scope anything.

Can you work with my existing theme?

Yes. We can customise the theme you already have, add new sections and features, or migrate you to a modern Online Store 2.0 theme while keeping the functionality your store depends on.

Do you build custom Shopify apps?

Yes. We build private, store-specific apps and integrations for logic an off-the-shelf app doesn't handle, such as custom workflows, data syncing or connections to your other business tools.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support and maintenance is one of our core services: fixes, improvements, performance work and new features as your store grows.

How do we get started?

Send us a message on Instagram with a short brief: your store, what you need and your timeline. We'll ask a few questions, recommend an approach and share an estimate before any work starts.
